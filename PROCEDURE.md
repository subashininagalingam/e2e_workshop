# Weekly procedure: from new workshop to certificates

Do these steps in order every week. Sign in at `/admin/` first.

## A. Before the workshop (about 10 minutes)

1. **Create the workshop.** Admin, Workshops, Add workshop. Fill title, description, poster, date and time (India time), mode, venue or meeting link, seat limit, topics, outcomes.
2. **Set this week's fee and payment QR.** Section *Fee and payment QR*: fee (for example 9) and either upload this week's QR image or enter the UPI ID (the site then makes the QR with the amount filled in). Leave "Fee is for the certificate" ticked so students choose Yes or No for a certificate; untick it if everyone must pay. Leave the fee blank for a free workshop.
3. **Publish.** Set Status to Published and tick Featured. Use the Preview link first if you want to look.
4. **Upload the certificate for this workshop.** Dashboard, Certificates, Upload certificate. Choose the blank template, adjust the name position until the preview looks right, type the first **Certificate ID** (for example `E2E/WS/2026/500`; the next ones are 501, 502...), place the ID, Save, then **Email me a sample certificate** and open it.
5. **Tell past students.** Dashboard, Announce, open the workshop. Write the subject and message, attach the brochure files, **Save and send me a test**, read it, then **Save and send next 40**. (People who already registered for this workshop are skipped, and nobody gets it twice.) Repeat the button until "Still to email" reaches 0 (Gmail limit is about 500 a day, so very large lists may need two days).
6. **Share the permanent QR** (Dashboard, QR codes). It never changes.

## B. While students register

7. A student fills the details and answers *Do you want a certificate?* **Yes**: the payment QR and amount appear, they pay, attach the screenshot and submit. **No**: they just submit. Either way they get a confirmation email, and the home page shows a countdown to the closing time.
7b. When the last seat is taken you get an email, and registration closes by itself. Raise the seat limit in the admin to take more.
8. Open Dashboard, **Students**, filter by workshop. For each student who wants a certificate, click **Screenshot**, check the amount, then tick **Payment verified**. If a student sent the screenshot by WhatsApp, use **Upload screenshot** on their row. Use the *Confirmation email* filter to find students whose email did not go. **Payments** shows the money totals per workshop. Use **Reject** if the payment is wrong. (Ticking only saves it. The certificate waits for feedback.)

## C. At the end of the workshop

9. Show the feedback address (`/feedback/`) or QR. Each student enters their name, the same email they registered with, feedback and a star rating.
10. The moment a student with a verified payment submits feedback, their certificate is emailed (PDF, with their name). A student whose payment you verify later gets it the moment you tick the box. Nothing else to do.
11. Check the Students page: the **Certificate** column says Sent. If it says Waiting, the text tells you what is missing (payment check, feedback). **Send anyway** overrides it for a special case. If an email failed, the row says why and **Send now** retries.

## D. After the workshop

12. Dashboard, Feedback: read it. **Hide** removes one from the website without deleting, **Delete** removes it for good.
13. Upload photos: Admin, open the workshop, add photos at the bottom (*Students at the workshop* or *Student feedback screenshot*). They appear on the home page and `/gallery/`.
14. Download Excel from Students and keep a backup copy.

## Rules the system follows

- A certificate is only sent when: template uploaded, workshop started, payment verified (or free), feedback received for the same email. It is sent **once**; only you can resend.
- The name on the certificate is the name typed at registration. Names typed in ALL CAPS or all lowercase are tidied to Title Case.
- Announcements go to students of earlier workshops only, once each, never to people who registered for the new workshop already, whose payment was rejected, or who unsubscribed. The meeting link is never in an announcement.
- Payment screenshots and certificate templates are private (not reachable by a public web address).
